About the Project

This project studies how Instagram influences the purchase decisions of college students through advertisements, influencers, content and product reviews.

The study helps to understand how Instagram marketing affects student preferences and buying behaviour.

Project Objectives

📱 Instagram Usage

To study how students use Instagram in their daily life.

🛍️ Product Discovery

To understand how students discover products through Instagram.

📢 Advertising Impact

To analyse the influence of Instagram advertisements on students.

⭐ Influencer Impact

To study the effect of influencers on purchase decisions.
