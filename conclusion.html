Study Conclusion

The study shows that Instagram has an important role in the buying behaviour of college students. Students use Instagram not only for entertainment but also to discover new products and brands.

Advertisements, influencers, Reels, product reviews and discounts can affect students' purchase decisions. Overall, Instagram has become an important platform for product discovery and digital marketing among college students.
