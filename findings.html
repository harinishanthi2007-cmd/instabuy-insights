🔎 Survey Analysis

Key Findings

Important findings from the survey of 152 college students on Instagram marketing and buying behaviour.

📱

Instagram Usage

64

Students use Instagram for 1–2 hours per day, making it the most common usage category.

🔍

Product Discovery

82

Students reported discovering new products through Instagram.

🛍️

Purchase Behaviour

66

Students purchased a product after seeing it on Instagram.

🎬

Influential Content

106

Reels were the most influential content type among the surveyed students.

💬

Reviews & Comments

62

Students always check comments or reviews before making a purchase.

🏷️

Discount Influence

54

Students said discounts sometimes influence their purchase decisions.

📊

Overall Finding

The survey indicates that Instagram plays an important role in product discovery and buying behaviour among college students.

Reels, advertisements, influencers, reviews and promotional offers are important elements of Instagram marketing.
